About ISM

Frisco ISD's Independent Study and Mentorship (ISM) program is a selective course for students who want to explore a career or topic they are genuinely interested in. Students research their chosen field, conduct interviews with professionals, and develop an Original Work based on their findings.

Throughout the program, students build real-world skills such as time management, communication, professionalism, and public speaking. In the spring semester, students work directly with a mentor at their place of business to gain firsthand experience and create a Final Product that applies their research in a meaningful way. By the end of the year, students present their work and leave ISM with a clearer understanding of their field and future goals.
